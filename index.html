💬 Objection Handling
❓
"Is it real gold/silver?"
"Most pieces are gold- or rhodium-plated — that keeps the price accessible while still looking beautiful. We also carry a fine line in solid 14K gold."
❓
"Will it tarnish?"
"Rhodium-plated pieces resist tarnishing well. Keep it away from water, lotion, and perfume. I tell clients: jewelry on last, off first."
❓
"It seems expensive."
"For real semi-precious stones, it's actually accessible — most pieces are under $80, from a brand celebrities wear."
❓
"Not sure which color."
"That's what the Color Bar is for! We can swap the stone in store so you see which feels most like you."
